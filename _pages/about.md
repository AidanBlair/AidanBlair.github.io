My name is Aidan Blair, I am a Research Assistant in the School of Computing Technologies at RMIT University in Melbourne, Australia. My research interests are multi-target tracking, multi-sensor control, distributed networks, computer vision, optimization, and machine learning. Please feel free to contact me via my email (aidan.blair@rmit.edu.au) if you have any questions!

## Projects
### Enhancing road safety through fleet-based vehicle sensor technology - NRSAG (2025-2026)
- Worked alongside industry partner MetaSpatial
- Developed novel information fusion methodologies for a fleet of multimodal sensor-equipped vehicles detecting road hazards

### Next-generation stress imaging for rapid structural prototyping, testing and evaluation - AEA Seed (2024-2025)
- Worked alongside industry partner 1MILLIKELVIN
- Developed and evaluated optimal camera placement algorithms for thermoelastic stress analysis, including simulation and experimental validation

### Using AI for cattle monitoring in feedlots - Food Agility CRC (2024)
- Helped implement long-range multi-camera calibration using Structure-from-Motion techniques

### Statistical information fusion for multi-camera visual tracking - PhD Thesis, ARC Scholarship (2020-2024)
- Developed novel distribute multi-sensor control algorithms in the Random Finite Set framework
- Developed novel Structure-from-Motion algorithms for a distributed network of camera-equipped vehicles using temporal information
