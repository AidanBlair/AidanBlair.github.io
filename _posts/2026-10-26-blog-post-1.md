It's an honour to see the project that we've been working on for the last year being shown on [ABC News](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=FNFoFT8b2HE) and [Channel 7](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=YvdD4xM0vX4)!

RoughSense is a scalable sensor suite that can be attached to any vehicle and will detect and map potholes and other degradations in the road surface over time.
